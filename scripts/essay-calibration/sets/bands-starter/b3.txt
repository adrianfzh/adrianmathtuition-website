A small act of kindness can make a big difference. I learnt this when I was in Secondary One.

On the first day of school, I did not know anybody in my class. All my primary school friends went to other schools and I was very nervous. During recess, everyone walked to the canteen in groups, but I walk alone. I bought a plate of chicken rice and looked for a seat. Every table was full of students who was talking and laughing. I stood there holding my plate and I feel very awkward.

Then a boy at one of the table waved at me. He said, "You can sit here, we have space." He moved his bag so I can sit down. His name was Jun Wei and he was from my class. He introduced me to his friends and they asked me which primary school I came from. We talked about games and football, I started to feel less nervous.

After that day, I always sat with them during recess. Jun Wei also helped me when I did not understand the Science homework. Because of him, I begin to enjoy going to school. I think if he did not call me that day, I will be very lonely for a long time, because I am a shy person and I do not dare to talk to people first.

It was only a small thing for him. He just said one sentence and moved his bag. But for me it was a very big thing, it made my first day better and gave me friends.

Now I am in Secondary Three. This year there is a new student in my class who came from another country. On his first day, I saw him standing in the canteen with his food, just like me last time. I remembered what Jun Wei did, so I waved at him and asked him to sit with us.

In conclusion, we should be kind to other people even in small ways, because we do not know how much it means to them.
