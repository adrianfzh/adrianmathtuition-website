When I was Secondary Two I learn that one small kind thing can change a person whole year. This is what happen to me and my neighbour uncle.

That time my father lost his job. He was drive lorry for a company for eleven years, then the company close down. At home everybody was quiet. My mother start to cook less food and my father sit at the corridor every evening and look at the carpark. I was worry but I don't know how to help, I am only fourteen.

Our neighbour, Uncle Rahim, he stay at the next door for many years but we only say hello. One evening he see my father sitting outside again. He did not ask any question. He just bring two cup of coffee and sit down beside my father. They never talk much. The next evening he come again, and the next one also. It was only coffee, it cost him maybe one dollar.

But after one week my father start to talk to him. He tell Uncle Rahim about the lorry and about how he scared he is too old to find new job. Uncle Rahim listen. Then he say his cousin company need a driver for delivery, the pay is not high but is honest work. He write the phone number on the coffee cup.

My father was shy to call. For two days the cup just stand on our table. I remember I look at that cup every time I do my homework. In the end my mother and me push him to call, and he got the job the same week.

After that my house was different. My father whistle again when he wear his shoes in the morning. My mother cook curry on Sunday and ask me to bring one bowl to Uncle Rahim. I see that the big difference was not only the job. Is that somebody sit with my father when he feel useless, so he was not alone with his problem.

Now every evening when I come back from school, if I see some neighbour sitting alone I will stop and talk for a while. I cannot give them a job. But I can give five minute, like Uncle Rahim give one cup of coffee, and maybe for them it is not small.
