One day I was go to school by bus. That day is raining very heavy and I forget to bring my umbrella. When I come down from the bus my uniform all wet already. I was very sad because I have test on that day and my paper will wet also.

Then one auntie she see me and she share her umbrella with me. She walk with me until the school gate. I say thank you to her and she say never mind. She is very kind. I don't know her name.

After that I go to my class. My friend ask me why you so wet and I tell him about the rain. Then teacher come in and we do the test. The test was very hard. I think I cannot pass because I never study the chapter five. After the test we go for recess and I eat noodles with my friend. The noodles was nice.

When I go home I tell my mother about the auntie. My mother say the auntie is good person and next time I must bring umbrella. So now I always bring umbrella in my bag everyday. Sometime it is heavy but I still bring.

I think kindness is important because it make people happy. If nobody is kind the world will be very bad place. So we must be kind to everyone like the auntie. That is the small act of kindness that I remember.
