Dear Mr Lim,

I am writing on behalf of the Secondary Four student council to propose a Reading Corner in the school canteen. We believe it would encourage more students to read during recess and after school.

At present, many students finish their meals within fifteen minutes and then have nothing to do. Most of them take out their phones, and a few simply wait for the bell. The library is on the fourth floor, so very few students visit it during a short break. A small corner in the canteen would bring books to the place where students already gather.

We suggest placing two shelves and four benches beside the drinks stall, where there is an unused space. The shelves could hold about two hundred books. Students would borrow a book by writing their name in a logbook, and they would return it within a week. The council is willing to check the logbook every Friday and to keep the corner tidy.

The cost would be low. The school librarian, Mrs Devi, has agreed to give us one hundred older books from the library. The remaining books could come from a donation drive in March, and each class would be asked to bring ten books. The benches can be borrowed from the school hall, which has more than it needs. We would therefore only need funds for the two shelves, which cost about three hundred dollars in total.

We believe the corner would benefit the school in two ways. Firstly, students who seldom visit the library would see books every day. Secondly, the donation drive would teach students to share what they no longer use.

We would be grateful if you could consider this proposal. The council would be happy to present the plan to you in person at a time that suits you.

Thank you for your time.

Yours sincerely,
Tan Wei Ling
President, Student Council
