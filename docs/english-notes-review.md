# English notes review — what we hold and what it is good for

6 Oct 2026. Adrian: "review them", then "let's do the english guidance from the 11 good notes".

The 46 English "notes" files sit in the private `paper-library` bucket (`subject='english'`,
`status='library'`). They are other people's work. **We learn from them to write our own
guidance. Nothing in them is copied to a student, and no model essay, script or phrase bank
is used at all.** The guidance written from them is [`english-guidance-draft.md`](english-guidance-draft.md).

The authority is the SEAB 1184 syllabus (`docs/rubrics/1184_y26_sy.pdf`), not the notes.

## ⚠ Correction to the first review (6 Oct 2026)

The first pass marked two notes down for a "wrong paper structure". The notes were right and
the review was wrong. The syllabus says:

- **Paper 1 Writing, 70 marks**: Editing 10 · Situational Writing 30 · Continuous Writing 30.
- Situational Writing = Task Fulfilment 10 + Language 20. Continuous Writing = Content 10 + Language 20.
- Editing is in **Paper 1**, not Paper 2.
- **Paper 4 Oral, 30 marks**: Planned Response 15 (a video clip, up to 2 minutes) + Spoken
  Interaction 15. There is **no reading aloud** in 1184.

So note 29's "70 marks, 10 / 30 / 30" and note 38's "Task Fulfilment 10 + Language 20" are
correct. Anything a reviewer said about "Content 20 + Language 30" or "50 marks" is not 1184.

## Not used, and why

- **Model essays, full scripts and phrase banks** (05, 09, 27, 42, the long samples in 08, 14, 21):
  they teach memorising, and a rehearsed answer scores badly.
- **Wrong advice**: "never use Yours faithfully" (39); "lift the words" for a language-use
  question (18); "lift most of the summary points" (43) and its editing key; "prompts are
  picture-based" (04); a clause "needs an object" (00).
- **IP school rubrics** (11, 37): their band marks are the school's own, never 1184's.
- **Copied news articles and reading packs** (10, 16, 30, 32, 34, 41, 23): not ours to use.
- **Files with a real person's details** (02, 26, 39) or a leaked-site stamp (19): never
  quoted, never shown.
- **Thin or off-level** (01, 03, 06, 12, 13, 15, 17, 20, 24, 25, 29, 31, 38, 40): nothing a
  better note does not already cover.

## Where the schools disagree — Adrian picks the house style

These are school habits, not SEAB rules. The syllabus marks purpose, audience, context, the
required points and the given information; it does not print a layout.

1. A comma after "Dear Mr Tan" and after "Yours sincerely" — ACS(I) uses commas, Fuchun uses none.
2. The title line in a formal letter — "RE: …" underlined and centred (ACS(I)), or a plain bold subject line.
3. Paragraphs — blocked with a line between them (ACS(I)), or indented.
4. Close of a formal e-mail — "Regards" (ACS(I)) or "Yours sincerely".

The draft uses: commas, a plain subject line, blocked paragraphs, "Yours sincerely" — and never
marks a student down for the other choice.
