# Sub-group naming vocabulary (mined 2026-09-02)

Source of truth for Layer 2 renames: Adrian's own teaching library at
`Dropbox/2 MATH/`. Mined by shallow `ls` of the class folders (file and folder
names are the vocabulary) plus four documents opened for section headings:
`3 REV AM Summary Notes.docx`, `E MATH FORMULA SHEET (O LEVEL).docx`,
`1009 Vectors Summary Table.docx`, `REV A Statistics Summary Notes.docx`.

Conventions observed in the library: **Title Case**, short chapter phrases,
sub-parts as "Chapter N Descriptor" (e.g. "Trigonometry 5 R-formula"), verbs
like *Solving / Forming / Making / Finding*, "-ize" spellings in Adrian's own
notes (Factorization, Rationalize). Names never carry TeX — they render as
plain text in the practice picker, worked-example decks and /notes headings.
