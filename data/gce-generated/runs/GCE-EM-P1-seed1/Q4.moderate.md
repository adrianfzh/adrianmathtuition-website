# PAPER 1, QUESTION 4 of 27, 2 MARKS

Written as a product of its prime factors, $1176 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 7^2$.
(a) Explain why $6 \times 1176$ is a perfect square. [1]
(b) Find the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $\frac{1176}{n}$ is a cube number. [1]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $6 \times 1176 = 2^4 \times 3^2 \times 7^2 = (2^2 \times 3 \times 7)^2 = 84^2$; every prime has an even index, so it is a perfect square.
(b) $n = 147$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) $6 \times 1176 = 2 \times 3 \times 2^3 \times 3 \times 7^2 = 2^4 \times 3^2 \times 7^2$.
Every prime factor now has an even index, so $6 \times 1176 = (2^2 \times 3 \times 7)^2 = 84^2$, which is a perfect square.

(b) For $\frac{1176}{n}$ to be a cube number, every prime in its factorisation must have an index that is a multiple of 3.
$1176 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 7^2$: the index of 2 is already 3, so the factors $3$ and $7^2$ must be divided out.
Smallest $n = 3 \times 7^2 = 147$, giving $\frac{1176}{147} = 2^3 = 8$, a cube number.
