# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a)(i) $15$ minutes
(a)(ii) $20$
(b) The median time in the first race (53 minutes) is less than in the second race (58 minutes), so on average the runners in the first race were faster. The interquartile range in the first race (15 minutes) is greater than in the second race (9 minutes), so the times in the first race were more spread out (less consistent).

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a)(i) From the plot, the lower quartile is 47 minutes and the upper quartile is 62 minutes.
Interquartile range $= 62 - 47 = 15$ minutes.

(a)(ii) 62 minutes is the upper quartile, so $25\%$ of the runners took more than 62 minutes.
Number of runners $= \frac{25}{100} \times 80 = 20$.

(b) From the plot, the median of the first race is 53 minutes.
Comment on the averages: the median of the first race (53 minutes) is less than the median of the second race (58 minutes), so on average the runners in the first race completed the race more quickly.
Comment on the spread: the interquartile range of the first race (15 minutes) is greater than that of the second race (9 minutes), so the times in the first race were more spread out; the times in the second race were more consistent.
