# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $\frac{5\pi}{6}$ radians
(b) $60\pi$ cm$^2$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) $180^\circ = \pi$ radians, so $150^\circ = \frac{150}{180} \times \pi = \frac{5\pi}{6}$ radians.
(b) Area of sector $= \frac{1}{2} r^2 \theta = \frac{1}{2} \times 12^2 \times \frac{5\pi}{6} = 72 \times \frac{5\pi}{6} = 60\pi$ cm$^2$.
(Equivalently, area $= \frac{150}{360} \times \pi \times 12^2 = \frac{5}{12} \times 144\pi = 60\pi$ cm$^2$.)
