A square tile has sides of length $(3x + 2)$ cm. A square hole with sides of length $(x - 1)$ cm is cut from the tile, where $x > 1$. The remaining part of the tile is called the frame.
(a) Show that the area of the frame is $(8x^2 + 14x + 3)$ cm$^2$. [2]
(b) The area of the frame is $63$ cm$^2$. Find the length of a side of the tile. [2]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $(3x+2)^2 - (x-1)^2 = 9x^2 + 12x + 4 - (x^2 - 2x + 1) = 8x^2 + 14x + 3$ (shown)
(b) $8$ cm

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Area of frame $=$ area of tile $-$ area of hole
$= (3x + 2)^2 - (x - 1)^2$
$= (9x^2 + 12x + 4) - (x^2 - 2x + 1)$
$= 9x^2 + 12x + 4 - x^2 + 2x - 1$
$= 8x^2 + 14x + 3$ (shown).
[Alternative by difference of two squares: $(3x + 2 - x + 1)(3x + 2 + x - 1) = (2x + 3)(4x + 1) = 8x^2 + 14x + 3$.]

(b) $8x^2 + 14x + 3 = 63$
$8x^2 + 14x - 60 = 0$
$4x^2 + 7x - 30 = 0$
$(4x + 15)(x - 2) = 0$
$x = 2$ or $x = -\frac{15}{4}$ (rejected, since $x > 1$).
Length of a side of the tile $= 3(2) + 2 = 8$ cm.
Check: tile $8 \times 8 = 64$ cm$^2$, hole $1 \times 1 = 1$ cm$^2$, frame $64 - 1 = 63$ cm$^2$.
