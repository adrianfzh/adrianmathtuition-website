(a) Rearrange the formula to make $p$ the subject. [3]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $p = \frac{r(q^2 + 2)}{q^2 - 1}$
(b) $p = 11$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Square both sides: $q^2 = \frac{p + 2r}{p - r}$.
Multiply by $(p - r)$: $q^2(p - r) = p + 2r$
$q^2 p - q^2 r = p + 2r$
Collect the terms in $p$ on one side: $q^2 p - p = 2r + q^2 r$
$p(q^2 - 1) = r(q^2 + 2)$
$p = \frac{r(q^2 + 2)}{q^2 - 1}$.

(b) $p = \frac{8(3^2 + 2)}{3^2 - 1} = \frac{8 \times 11}{8} = 11$.
Check: $\sqrt{\frac{11 + 16}{11 - 8}} = \sqrt{9} = 3$.
