# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $MN^2 = 120^2 + 85^2 - 2(120)(85)\cos 104^\circ = 26560.2\ldots$, so $MN = 162.97\ldots = 163$ m (3 s.f.)
(b) $30.4^\circ$
(c) $60.7$ m

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) By the cosine rule in triangle $LMN$,
$MN^2 = LM^2 + LN^2 - 2(LM)(LN)\cos MLN$
$= 120^2 + 85^2 - 2(120)(85)\cos 104^\circ$
$= 14400 + 7225 - 20400(-0.24192\ldots)$
$= 21625 + 4935.2\ldots = 26560.2\ldots$
$MN = \sqrt{26560.2\ldots} = 162.97\ldots = 163$ m (correct to 3 significant figures).

(b) By the sine rule,
$\frac{\sin LMN}{LN} = \frac{\sin MLN}{MN}$
$\sin LMN = \frac{85 \sin 104^\circ}{162.97\ldots} = 0.50607\ldots$
Angle $LMN = 30.40\ldots^\circ = 30.4^\circ$ (1 d.p.).
(Using $MN = 163$ gives $\sin LMN = 0.50598\ldots$ and the same $30.4^\circ$.)

(c) The shortest path from $L$ to the road is the perpendicular from $L$ to $MN$. Let its foot be $P$; since the angles at $M$ and $N$ are both acute ($30.4^\circ$ and $180^\circ - 104^\circ - 30.4^\circ = 45.6^\circ$), $P$ lies between $M$ and $N$.
In right-angled triangle $LPM$, $LP = LM \sin LMN = 120 \sin 30.40\ldots^\circ = 60.73\ldots = 60.7$ m.

Alternatively, using the area: area of triangle $LMN = \frac{1}{2}(120)(85)\sin 104^\circ = 4948.5\ldots$ m$^2$, and also $= \frac{1}{2}(MN)(LP)$, so $LP = \frac{2 \times 4948.5\ldots}{162.97\ldots} = 60.7$ m.

Length of the path $= 60.7$ m.
