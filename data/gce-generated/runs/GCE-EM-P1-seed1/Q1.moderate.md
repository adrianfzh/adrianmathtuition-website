Solve the equation $\frac{5k - 2}{4} = 2k - 5$.

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
$k = 6$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
Multiply both sides by 4:
$5k - 2 = 4(2k - 5)$
$5k - 2 = 8k - 20$
Collect terms: $20 - 2 = 8k - 5k$, so $3k = 18$.
Hence $k = 6$.
Check: LHS $= \frac{5(6) - 2}{4} = \frac{28}{4} = 7$; RHS $= 2(6) - 5 = 7$.
