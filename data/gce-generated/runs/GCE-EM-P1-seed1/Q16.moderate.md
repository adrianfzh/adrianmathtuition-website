# PAPER 1, QUESTION 16 of 27, 3 MARKS

Two solid trophies are geometrically similar and are made of the same metal.
The smaller trophy has height $6$ cm and the larger trophy has height $15$ cm.
(a) The surface area of the smaller trophy is $72$ cm$^2$.
Calculate the surface area of the larger trophy. [1]
(b) The mass of the larger trophy is $875$ g.
Calculate the mass of the smaller trophy. [2]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $450$ cm$^2$
(b) $56$ g

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
Scale factor of lengths, smaller to larger: $k = \frac{15}{6} = \frac{5}{2} = 2.5$.

(a) Ratio of surface areas $= k^2 = \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{25}{4}$.
Surface area of larger trophy $= 72 \times \frac{25}{4} = 450$ cm$^2$.

(b) Ratio of volumes $= k^3 = \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{125}{8}$.
The trophies are made of the same metal, so mass is proportional to volume.
Mass of smaller trophy $= 875 \div \frac{125}{8} = 875 \times \frac{8}{125} = 7 \times 8 = 56$ g.
