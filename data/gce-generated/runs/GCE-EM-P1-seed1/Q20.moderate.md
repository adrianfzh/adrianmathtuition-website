Simplify $\frac{6x^2 - 7x - 20}{4x^2 - 25}$.

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
$\frac{3x + 4}{2x + 5}$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
Factorise the numerator (a quadratic trinomial): we need two numbers with product $6 \times (-20) = -120$ and sum $-7$; these are $-15$ and $8$.
$6x^2 - 7x - 20 = 6x^2 - 15x + 8x - 20 = 3x(2x - 5) + 4(2x - 5) = (3x + 4)(2x - 5)$

Factorise the denominator (difference of two squares):
$4x^2 - 25 = (2x)^2 - 5^2 = (2x - 5)(2x + 5)$

Cancel the common factor $(2x - 5)$:
$\frac{6x^2 - 7x - 20}{4x^2 - 25} = \frac{(3x + 4)(2x - 5)}{(2x - 5)(2x + 5)} = \frac{3x + 4}{2x + 5}$

Check by expanding: $(3x + 4)(2x - 5) = 6x^2 - 15x + 8x - 20 = 6x^2 - 7x - 20$ and $(2x - 5)(2x + 5) = 4x^2 - 25$.
