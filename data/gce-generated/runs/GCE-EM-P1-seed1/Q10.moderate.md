# PAPER 1, QUESTION 10 of 27, 4 MARKS

A paint shop makes a shade of paint called Sea Mist by mixing blue paint, white paint and grey paint in the ratio $2 : 9 : 4$ by volume.
(a) Find the volume of white paint in 7.5 litres of Sea Mist. [1]
(b) Express the ratio   volume of grey paint : volume of Sea Mist   in the form $1 : n$. [1]
(c) A decorator has 1.5 litres of blue paint, 8 litres of white paint and 2.6 litres of grey paint.
Find the greatest volume of Sea Mist she can make. [2]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) 4.5 litres
(b) $1 : 3.75$
(c) 9.75 litres

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Total number of parts $= 2 + 9 + 4 = 15$.
Volume of white paint $= \frac{9}{15} \times 7.5 = 4.5$ litres.

(b) Grey : Sea Mist $= 4 : 15 = 1 : \frac{15}{4} = 1 : 3.75$.

(c) Each batch of 15 parts needs 2 parts blue, 9 parts white and 4 parts grey.
Volume of one part allowed by each colour:
blue: $1.5 \div 2 = 0.75$ litres,
white: $8 \div 9 = 0.888\ldots$ litres,
grey: $2.6 \div 4 = 0.65$ litres.
The grey paint runs out first, so one part is at most $0.65$ litres.
Greatest volume of Sea Mist $= 15 \times 0.65 = 9.75$ litres.
(Check: this uses $2 \times 0.65 = 1.3$ litres of blue and $9 \times 0.65 = 5.85$ litres of white, both available.)
