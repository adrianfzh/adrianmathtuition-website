# PAPER 1, QUESTION 26 of 27, 5 MARKS

The diagram shows a solid wooden ornament.
It is made from a cylinder of radius 6 cm and height 10 cm.
A hemisphere of radius 4 cm is removed from the top face of the cylinder, as shown.
(a) Calculate the volume of the ornament. [2]
(b) Calculate the total surface area of the ornament.
Give your answer in terms of $\pi$. [3]

[The diagram, described in words — the printed paper shows it as a figure: A three-dimensional line drawing of an upright solid cylinder (circular faces drawn as ellipses, the hidden half of the bottom ellipse dashed). On the top face a smaller concentric ellipse marks the rim of a hemispherical hollow, with the lower curve of the hollow drawn dashed below the top face to show it is scooped out of the solid. Labels printed: a radius line from the centre of the top face to its rim labelled '6 cm'; a vertical line beside the cylinder labelled '10 cm' for its height; a radius line from the centre of the top face to the rim of the hollow labelled '4 cm'. Nothing else is printed — no volume, no surface area, no other lengths.]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $997$ cm$^3$ (exact value $\frac{952\pi}{3}$ cm$^3$)
(b) $208\pi$ cm$^2$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Volume of cylinder $= \pi \times 6^2 \times 10 = 360\pi$ cm$^3$.
Volume of hemisphere removed $= \frac{2}{3}\pi \times 4^3 = \frac{128\pi}{3}$ cm$^3$.
Volume of ornament $= 360\pi - \frac{128\pi}{3} = \frac{952\pi}{3} = 996.9\ldots \approx 997$ cm$^3$.

(b) The surface of the ornament has four pieces.
Curved surface of cylinder $= 2\pi \times 6 \times 10 = 120\pi$ cm$^2$.
Bottom face $= \pi \times 6^2 = 36\pi$ cm$^2$.
Top face, which is a circle of radius 6 cm with a circle of radius 4 cm removed $= \pi \times 6^2 - \pi \times 4^2 = 20\pi$ cm$^2$.
Curved surface of the hollow hemisphere $= 2\pi \times 4^2 = 32\pi$ cm$^2$.
Total surface area $= 120\pi + 36\pi + 20\pi + 32\pi = 208\pi$ cm$^2$.
