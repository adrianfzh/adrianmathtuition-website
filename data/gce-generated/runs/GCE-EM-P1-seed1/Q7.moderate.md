# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $AB = 6.0$ cm on the drawing; actual length $= 30$ m (accept 29.5 m to 30.5 m)
(b) Perpendicular bisector of $AB$ drawn with construction arcs (a line parallel to $BC$, 3.0 cm from $BC$ on the drawing)
(c) Bisector of angle $ABC$ drawn with construction arcs (the line through $B$ at $45^\circ$ to $BC$)
(d) $T$ marked at the intersection of the two bisectors; $TC = 5.0$ cm on the drawing, so actual distance $TC = 25$ m (accept 24.5 m to 25.5 m)

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) On the drawing $AB$ measures $6.0$ cm.
Actual length of $AB = 6.0 \times 5 = 30$ m.

(b) With compasses, arcs of equal radius (more than half of $AB$) centred at $A$ and at $B$ cross on either side of $AB$; the line through the two crossings is the perpendicular bisector of $AB$. Because angle $ABC = 90^\circ$, it is parallel to $BC$ and 3.0 cm above $BC$ on the drawing.

(c) With compasses, an arc centred at $B$ cuts $BA$ and $BC$; equal arcs centred at these two points cross inside the angle; the line from $B$ through the crossing is the bisector of angle $ABC$. It makes $45^\circ$ with $BC$.

(d) $T$ is equidistant from $A$ and $B$, so $T$ lies on the perpendicular bisector of $AB$. $T$ is equidistant from $AB$ and $BC$, so $T$ lies on the bisector of angle $ABC$. $T$ is the point where the two constructed lines meet: on the drawing it is 3.0 cm from $AB$ and 3.0 cm from $BC$.
Measuring, $TC = 5.0$ cm.
Actual distance $TC = 5.0 \times 5 = 25$ m.
(Check: in metres $T$ is 15 m from $AB$ and 15 m from $BC$, so with $N$ the foot of the perpendicular from $T$ to $BC$, $BN = 15$ m, $TN = 15$ m, $NC = 35 - 15 = 20$ m and $TC = \sqrt{20^2 + 15^2} = \sqrt{625} = 25$ m, which agrees with the measurement.)
