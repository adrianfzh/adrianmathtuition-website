# PAPER 1, QUESTION 5 of 27, 3 MARKS

Priya invests $\$5600$ at a rate of $2.5\%$ per year compound interest.
(a) Calculate the interest earned during the second year. [2]
(b) Priya says that the total interest earned in the first 8 years will be $\$1120$. Explain why the total interest will be more than $\$1120$. [1]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $\$143.50$
(b) $\$1120 = 5600 \times \frac{2.5}{100} \times 8$ is SIMPLE interest, calculated on $\$5600$ every year. With compound interest each year's interest is calculated on the growing amount, so from the second year onwards the yearly interest is more than $\$140$ (the second year alone earns $\$143.50$), and the total is more than $\$1120$.

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Amount at the end of the first year $= 5600 \times 1.025 = \$5740$.
Interest earned during the second year $= 5740 \times \frac{2.5}{100} = \$143.50$.
(Alternatively: $5600 \times 1.025^2 - 5600 \times 1.025 = 5883.50 - 5740 = \$143.50$.)

(b) $5600 \times \frac{2.5}{100} \times 8 = \$1120$ is the interest if it were SIMPLE interest, calculated on the original $\$5600$ in every one of the 8 years.
With compound interest each year's interest is calculated on the growing amount, so the interest in every year after the first is more than $\$140$ (the second year earns $\$143.50$ from part (a)). Hence the total interest is more than $\$1120$.
(For reference, the actual total is $5600 \times 1.025^8 - 5600 \approx \$1223.06$.)
