# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $32^\circ$
(b) $148^\circ$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) In a parallelogram, adjacent angles are supplementary (co-interior angles, $AD \parallel BC$).
Angle $DAB = 180^\circ - 116^\circ = 64^\circ$
$AE$ bisects angle $DAB$, so angle $DAE = \frac{1}{2} \times 64^\circ = 32^\circ$.

(b) Angle $EAB = 32^\circ$ (the other half of the bisected angle).
Since $DC \parallel AB$, angle $DEA = $ angle $EAB = 32^\circ$ (alternate angles).
Angle $AEC = 180^\circ - 32^\circ = 148^\circ$ (angles on a straight line, $DEC$).

Alternative for (b): angle $ADE = $ angle $ABC = 116^\circ$ (opposite angles of a parallelogram), so in triangle $ADE$ angle $AED = 180^\circ - 116^\circ - 32^\circ = 32^\circ$, giving angle $AEC = 148^\circ$.

(Setter's check of the figure: triangle $ADE$ has angle $DAE = $ angle $DEA = 32^\circ$, so $DE = DA = 5$ units; with $DC = 9$ units, $E$ lies strictly between $D$ and $C$, as drawn.)
