# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
Angle $PQR = 122^\circ$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
Reflex angle $POR = 360^\circ - 116^\circ = 244^\circ$ (angles at a point).

Angle $PQR = \frac{1}{2} \times 244^\circ = 122^\circ$ (the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference standing on the same arc; $Q$ is on the minor arc $PR$, so angle $PQR$ stands on the major arc $PR$, which subtends the reflex angle at $O$).

Alternatively: take any point $S$ on the major arc $PR$. Angle $PSR = \frac{1}{2} \times 116^\circ = 58^\circ$ (angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference), and angle $PQR = 180^\circ - 58^\circ = 122^\circ$ (opposite angles of the cyclic quadrilateral $PQRS$ are supplementary).

Angle $PQR = 122^\circ$.
