The cash price of an electric bicycle is $\$1840$.
Under a hire-purchase scheme, a customer pays a deposit of $\$400$ followed by 18 equal monthly payments.
The hire-purchase price is $22.5\%$ more than the cash price.
(a) Calculate the hire-purchase price. [1]
(b) Calculate the amount of each monthly payment. [2]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $\$2254$
(b) $\$103$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Hire-purchase price $= 1840 \times \frac{122.5}{100} = 1840 \times 1.225 = \$2254$.
(b) Total of the monthly payments $= 2254 - 400 = \$1854$.
Each monthly payment $= \frac{1854}{18} = \$103$.
