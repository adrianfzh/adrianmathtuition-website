Two shops, Shop A and Shop B, sell bags of rice, trays of eggs and bottles of cooking oil. The matrix $\mathbf{P}$ shows the price, in dollars, of each item at each shop. The rows represent Shop A and Shop B, and the columns represent rice, eggs and cooking oil, in that order.
$\mathbf{P} = \begin{pmatrix} 12 & 4 & 7 \\ 13 & 3 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$
Mrs Tan needs to buy 2 bags of rice, 5 trays of eggs and 3 bottles of cooking oil.
(a) Represent the numbers of items Mrs Tan needs to buy in a $3 \times 1$ matrix $\mathbf{Q}$. [1]
(b) Find the matrix $\mathbf{C} = \mathbf{PQ}$. [2]
(c) Find the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}\mathbf{C}$ and explain what the element of this matrix represents. [1]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $\mathbf{Q} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$
(b) $\mathbf{C} = \begin{pmatrix} 65 \\ 59 \end{pmatrix}$
(c) $\begin{pmatrix} 6 \end{pmatrix}$; buying all the items at Shop A costs $\$6$ more than buying all of them at Shop B.

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) The rows of $\mathbf{Q}$ must match the columns of $\mathbf{P}$ (rice, eggs, cooking oil).
$\mathbf{Q} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix}$

(b) $\mathbf{C} = \mathbf{PQ} = \begin{pmatrix} 12 & 4 & 7 \\ 13 & 3 & 6 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 5 \\ 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 12 \times 2 + 4 \times 5 + 7 \times 3 \\ 13 \times 2 + 3 \times 5 + 6 \times 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 24 + 20 + 21 \\ 26 + 15 + 18 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 65 \\ 59 \end{pmatrix}$
(The elements are the total cost, in dollars, of all the items at Shop A and at Shop B respectively.)

(c) $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 65 \\ 59 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 65 - 59 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \end{pmatrix}$
The element is the difference between the two totals: buying all the items at Shop A costs $\$6$ more than buying all of them at Shop B.
