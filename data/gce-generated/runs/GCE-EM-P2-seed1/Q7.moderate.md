The table shows the distribution of the distances, $d$ km, cycled by the members of a cycling club in one week.

$\begin{array}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|} \hline \text{Distance } (d \text{ km}) & 0 < d \leqslant 20 & 20 < d \leqslant 40 & 40 < d \leqslant 60 & 60 < d \leqslant 80 & 80 < d \leqslant 100 \\ \hline \text{Frequency} & 5 & x & 18 & 13 & 7 \\ \hline \end{array}$

The estimated mean distance cycled is 50 km.
(a) Find the value of $x$. [2]
(b) Calculate an estimate of the standard deviation of the distances. [2]
(c)  [4]
  (i) One of the members is chosen at random. Find the probability that this member cycled more than 60 km. [1]
  (ii) Two of the members are chosen at random. Find the probability that exactly one of them cycled more than 60 km. Give your answer as a fraction in its lowest terms. [3]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) $x = 17$
(b) 22.8 km
(c)(i) $\frac{1}{3}$
(c)(ii) $\frac{80}{177}$

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Mid-values 10, 30, 50, 70, 90.
$\sum f = 5 + x + 18 + 13 + 7 = 43 + x$
$\sum fx = 5(10) + 30x + 18(50) + 13(70) + 7(90) = 50 + 30x + 900 + 910 + 630 = 2490 + 30x$
Estimated mean $= \frac{2490 + 30x}{43 + x} = 50$
$2490 + 30x = 2150 + 50x$
$20x = 340$
$x = 17$

(b) Total frequency $= 43 + 17 = 60$.
$\sum fx^2 = 5(100) + 17(900) + 18(2500) + 13(4900) + 7(8100) = 500 + 15300 + 45000 + 63700 + 56700 = 181200$
Standard deviation $= \sqrt{\frac{181200}{60} - 50^2} = \sqrt{3020 - 2500} = \sqrt{520} = 22.803\ldots = 22.8$ km (3 s.f.).

(c)(i) Number who cycled more than 60 km $= 13 + 7 = 20$ out of 60.
$P(\text{more than 60 km}) = \frac{20}{60} = \frac{1}{3}$.

(c)(ii) Selection is without replacement. 20 members cycled more than 60 km and 40 did not.
$P(\text{exactly one}) = P(\text{more, not more}) + P(\text{not more, more}) = \frac{20}{60} \times \frac{40}{59} + \frac{40}{60} \times \frac{20}{59} = 2 \times \frac{800}{3540} = \frac{1600}{3540} = \frac{80}{177}$.
