  (i) Find the value of $x$. [1]

[2] GCE 2024 P2 Q6 (11 marks; topics: Algebra (Quadratic Equations), Numbers (Speed))
Nadia drives the first 40 km of a journey on an expressway and the remaining 20 km on local roads. She drives on the expressway at an average speed of $x$ km/h. Her average speed on the local roads is 30 km/h slower than her average speed on the expressway. The journey takes Nadia a total of 50 minutes.
(a) Show that $x^2 - 102x + 1440 = 0$. [5]
(b) Solve the equation $x^2 - 102x + 1440 = 0$. Give your solutions correct to two decimal places. [3]
(c) Explain why one of the solutions in part (b) must be rejected. [1]
(d) Calculate the difference between the times Nadia spent driving on the expressway and driving on local roads. Give your answer in minutes and seconds, correct to the nearest second. [2]
