# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
The calculator is set to radians for every step that uses the sector angle.

(a) In triangle $OAC$, $OA = 9$ cm, $OC = 5$ cm and the included angle $AOC = 1.2$ radians.
By the cosine rule,
$AC^{2} = 9^{2} + 5^{2} - 2(9)(5)\cos 1.2$
$= 106 - 90(0.362357\ldots)$
$= 106 - 32.6121\ldots$
$= 73.3878\ldots$
$AC = \sqrt{73.3878\ldots} = 8.56666\ldots = 8.57$ cm (3 s.f.)

(b) In triangle $OAC$, by the sine rule,
$\frac{\sin OAC}{OC} = \frac{\sin AOC}{AC}$
$\sin OAC = \frac{5 \sin 1.2}{8.56666\ldots} = \frac{5(0.932039\ldots)}{8.56666\ldots} = 0.543991\ldots$
Angle $OAC$ is opposite the shortest side $OC$, so it is acute.
Angle $OAC = \sin^{-1}(0.543991\ldots) = 32.9557\ldots^\circ = 33.0^\circ$ (1 d.p.)

(c)(i) Arc $AB = r\theta = 9 \times 1.2 = 10.8$ cm
$CB = OB - OC = 9 - 5 = 4$ cm
Perimeter of shaded region $=$ arc $AB + CB + AC$
$= 10.8 + 4 + 8.56666\ldots$
$= 23.3666\ldots = 23.4$ cm (3 s.f.)

(c)(ii) Area of sector $OAB = \frac{1}{2}r^{2}\theta = \frac{1}{2}(9^{2})(1.2) = 48.6$ cm$^{2}$
Area of triangle $OAC = \frac{1}{2}(OA)(OC)\sin AOC = \frac{1}{2}(9)(5)\sin 1.2 = 20.9708\ldots$ cm$^{2}$
Shaded area $= 48.6 - 20.9708\ldots = 27.6291\ldots = 27.6$ cm$^{2}$ (3 s.f.)
