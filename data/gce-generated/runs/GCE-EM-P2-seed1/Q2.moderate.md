Mei runs a small café.
(a) Mei buys a coffee machine from Italy for €2 450. The exchange rate is €1 = S$1.48.
Goods and Services Tax (GST) of 9% is charged on the price in Singapore dollars.
Calculate the total amount, in Singapore dollars, that Mei pays for the machine. [2]
(b) Mei increased the selling price of a cup of latte by 8%. The new selling price is $\$5.94$.
Calculate the selling price of a cup of latte before the increase. [2]
(c) The cost of making a cup of latte is made up of the cost of coffee beans, the cost of milk and other costs in the ratio $5 : 4 : 3$. The coffee beans for one cup cost $\$1.25$.
Calculate the percentage profit Mei makes on a cup of latte sold at $\$5.94$. [2]
(d) Mei spends $\$180$ on Grade A coffee beans, which cost $\$x$ per kilogram.
Grade B coffee beans cost $\$5$ per kilogram more than Grade A coffee beans.
For $\$180$, Mei would get 3 kg less of Grade B coffee beans than of Grade A coffee beans. [5]
  (i) Form an equation in $x$ and show that it simplifies to $x^2 + 5x - 300 = 0$. [3]
  (ii) Solve the equation $x^2 + 5x - 300 = 0$. [1]
  (iii) Hence find the cost of 1 kilogram of Grade B coffee beans. [1]

# SETTER'S ANSWER KEY
(a) S$3952.34
(b) $\$5.50$
(c) $98\%$
(d)(i) $\frac{180}{x} - \frac{180}{x+5} = 3$, which simplifies to $x^2 + 5x - 300 = 0$ (shown)
(d)(ii) $x = 15$ or $x = -20$
(d)(iii) $\$20$ (taking $x = 15$; $x = -20$ rejected as a price cannot be negative)

# SETTER'S WORKED SOLUTION
(a) Price in Singapore dollars $= 2450 \times 1.48 = \$3626$.
With GST, total paid $= 3626 \times 1.09 = \$3952.34$.

(b) The new price is $108\%$ of the old price.
$108\% \to \$5.94$, so $100\% \to \frac{5.94}{1.08} = \$5.50$.

(c) Beans are 5 parts of the ratio $5 : 4 : 3$, which has 12 parts in all.
5 parts $= \$1.25$, so 12 parts $= \frac{1.25}{5} \times 12 = \$3.00$ (the cost of making a cup).
Profit per cup $= 5.94 - 3.00 = \$2.94$.
Percentage profit $= \frac{2.94}{3.00} \times 100\% = 98\%$.

(d)(i) Mass of Grade A beans bought for $\$180$ $= \frac{180}{x}$ kg.
Grade B beans cost $\$(x+5)$ per kilogram, so the mass of Grade B beans bought for $\$180$ $= \frac{180}{x+5}$ kg.
The Grade B mass is 3 kg less: $\frac{180}{x} - \frac{180}{x+5} = 3$.
Multiply through by $x(x+5)$: $180(x+5) - 180x = 3x(x+5)$
$180x + 900 - 180x = 3x^2 + 15x$
$900 = 3x^2 + 15x$
Divide by 3: $300 = x^2 + 5x$, so $x^2 + 5x - 300 = 0$ (shown).

(d)(ii) $x^2 + 5x - 300 = 0$
$(x - 15)(x + 20) = 0$
$x = 15$ or $x = -20$.

(d)(iii) $x$ is a price per kilogram, so $x = -20$ is rejected and $x = 15$.
Cost of 1 kg of Grade B beans $= 15 + 5 = \$20$.
(Check: $\$180$ buys $\frac{180}{15} = 12$ kg of Grade A and $\frac{180}{20} = 9$ kg of Grade B; $12 - 9 = 3$ kg.)
