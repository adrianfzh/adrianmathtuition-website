# QUESTION 9 (10 marks)

Mr Tan is deciding whether to install a rainwater tank to water the plants in his garden.
He finds the following information.

Rainwater tank
The tank is a cylinder with internal diameter $80$ cm and internal height $120$ cm.
Price, including installation: $\$420$
Expected lifespan: $10$ years

Collecting rainwater
Rain falling on the roof of the garden shed drains into the tank.
The roof is a rectangle $3.6$ m by $2.5$ m.
$80\%$ of the rain that falls on the roof reaches the tank.
Average rainfall: $175$ mm per month

Watering the garden
Mr Tan waters his garden with a hose for $12$ minutes every day.
The hose delivers $9$ litres of water per minute.

Monthly water tariff
$\begin{array}{|l|c|} \hline \text{First } 40 \text{ m}^3 & \$2.74 \text{ per m}^3 \\ \hline \text{Each m}^3 \text{ above } 40 \text{ m}^3 & \$3.69 \text{ per m}^3 \\ \hline \end{array}$

$1 \text{ m}^3 = 1000$ litres
(a) Calculate the capacity of the tank.
Give your answer in litres. [2]
(b) In a month with $30$ days, calculate [3]
  (i) the volume of water, in litres, Mr Tan uses to water his garden, [1]
  (ii) the volume of rainwater, in litres, that reaches the tank. [2]
(c) Before installing the tank, Mr Tan's household uses $43 \text{ m}^3$ of tap water each month.
Assume that all the rainwater that reaches the tank is used to water the garden instead of tap water.
Is it worth Mr Tan installing the rainwater tank?
Justify your answer with calculations. [5]
